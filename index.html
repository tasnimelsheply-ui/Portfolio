How I work

Understand the contextI look at where the data comes from and why values might be wrong, not only at the columns.
Clean with logicEvery cleaning decision (IQR, known ranges, imputation) has a reason tied to the column.
Compare before and afterNo cleaning step ships without proof that it changed the shape of the data.
